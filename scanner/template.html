How the numbers are made

  • Entry: a real Pendle router quote from a USD stable into the PT, aggregator route included, at $100, $1k, $10k and $50k. Other sizes are interpolated between those quotes. A quote is never allowed to beat the pool's mid price.
  • At maturity: each PT redeems for one unit of the asset, valued at its current USD price, minus the cost of converting back to a stable and the gas to redeem.
  • All-in APY annualises the period return. For very short maturities a small cost moves it a lot, so read the dollar profit too.
  • Edge is all-in APY minus the better of the asset's own floating yield and the Aave USDC rate. Meets rule means an edge of at least 1 point and at least 2 points over cash. That is the entry rule from the 2024-2026 backtest.
  • Leverage uses the cheapest listed lending market for that PT (7-day average borrow rate), entry quotes at the levered size, loop gas, and holds to maturity. Health factor is approximate.

Before you trade

  • This is a snapshot. Rates and liquidity move by the minute, so check the live quote in Pendle before buying.
  • A PT pays its fixed rate only if the asset holds its value until maturity. Several high-yield names are young synthetic dollars, and a depeg or default loses principal.
  • "Points-priced" markets price in points or airdrops that the PT holder gives up. The fixed rate is real, but there is no floating yield to compare it with.
  • Leverage multiplies both outcomes. Morpho PT loops have been liquidated in minutes, so keep the health factor well above 1.
  • Nothing here is financial advice.
